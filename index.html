Case studies

Enterprise systems built under real constraints — regulation, privacy, scale and explainability. Written as Problem → Approach → Impact → Stack.

Johnson & Johnson · Knowledge Graphs · XAI

Enterprise knowledge graph foundation for explainable AI

Problem
Supplier, manufacturing, batch-release and stability-study data lived in separate domains, making it hard to build AI whose answers could be explained and governed in a GxP-regulated setting.
Approach
Led ontology design and production deployment of domain-specific knowledge graphs on Dgraph across supplier data, drug manufacturing, batch release and stability studies — a shared semantic backbone that downstream AI can reason over and cite.
Impact
Established a scalable, production knowledge-graph backbone for explainable, governed AI use cases across quality and supply chain.
  • Dgraph
  • Ontology design
  • Knowledge graphs
  • XAI
  • Data governance

Johnson & Johnson · Agentic AI

Agentic regulatory document intelligence + agent-to-agent (A2A) interoperability

Problem
Business users spent heavy manual effort navigating quality and supplier documentation, and complex data-synthesis workflows spanned internal systems and third-party vendors.
Approach
Built autonomous document-intelligence agents that navigate, extract, synthesize and summarize documentation. Designed a secure agent-to-agent communication protocol connecting internal AI agents with third-party vendor agents under governance controls.
Impact
Accelerated knowledge discovery, reduced manual research effort, and enabled governed multi-agent automation for enterprise data synthesis.
  • Multi-agent systems
  • A2A protocol
  • LLMs
  • Document intelligence
  • Governance

Johnson & Johnson · RAG · GxP

RAG pipelines for GxP document intelligence

Problem
Supplier qualifications, lab specifications and drug product/substance documents are dense, regulated, and costly to search — answers must be grounded in the source.
Approach
Developed end-to-end retrieval pipelines: indexing, chunking strategy, embedding-based retrieval and context optimization, with grounding and hallucination mitigation for regulated knowledge access.
Impact
Grounded, regulated knowledge access over GxP documentation for quality and supply-chain teams.
  • RAG
  • Embeddings
  • Semantic search
  • Chunking
  • Grounding

Johnson & Johnson · Applied ML

ML for quality-issue resolution & privacy-safe synthetic data

Problem
Manufacturing deviations and supplier incidents were triaged manually, and sensitive supply-chain data slowed model development and testing.
Approach
Built ML classifiers for deviations and supplier incidents; integrated synthetic tabular data generation for supply-chain risk modeling and testing; PySpark/Azure Databricks pipelines to cleanse, standardize and validate batch-quality and logistics records.
Impact
−40% manual investigation time, +15% incident identification accuracy, −40% manual review time, faster development while preserving data privacy.
  • Classification
  • Synthetic data
  • PySpark
  • Azure Databricks
  • Data quality

Comcast · Real-time LLMs · MLOps

Real-time LLM retention platform with a feedback-driven retraining loop

Problem
Retention offers needed to be personalized in the moment, while live customer conversations and frontline feedback kept shifting.
Approach
Designed, trained and deployed an LLM-powered platform that analyzes live call and chat transcripts to generate personalized offers, backed by high-cardinality churn models with MLflow, Databricks and a feature store. Closed the loop with Databricks Workflows feeding real-time agent ratings into weekly fine-tuning cycles.
Impact
+20% offer acceptance, with recommendation relevance improving as customer behavior and feedback evolved.
  • LLMs
  • Fine-tuning
  • MLflow
  • Databricks Workflows
  • Feature store

Comcast · Streaming inference

Streaming LLM inference for proactive routing & root-cause classification

Problem
Escalations and long handle times came from signals in call and chat telemetry that weren't acted on in time.
Approach
Architected streaming ML pipelines that turn sentiment, intent and escalation signals into near-real-time routing decisions. Productionized CI/CD for sentiment and complaint-categorization models (automated labeling, retraining, deployment), shipped on Docker/Kubernetes with blue/green releases, A/B testing and monitoring.
Impact
−18% average handle time, −30% escalations, 95% accuracy in primary complaint-driver detection.
  • Streaming pipelines
  • NLP
  • CI/CD
  • Kubernetes
  • A/B testing

Regulatory Data Corp · Financial crime · Explainability

AML anomaly detection & explainable fraud models with SHAP

Problem
Fraud and compliance teams were buried in false positives from high-velocity transaction streams, and regulators require transparency into why activity is flagged.
Approach
Real-time pipelines in Python, PySpark and AWS Lambda; unsupervised AML risk scoring with Delta Lake and Kubeflow Pipelines for automated retraining; Dockerized ensemble fraud models with CI/CD and SHAP-based interpretability; deep-learning NER and sequence classification on unstructured transaction descriptions.
Impact
−25% false positives (AML scoring), −28% false positives and +19% early fraud detection (ensembles), +23% previously undetected suspicious entities identified.
  • Anomaly detection
  • SHAP
  • Kubeflow
  • AWS Lambda
  • Delta Lake
  • NER

How I work

Engineering principles

  • ◆

    Production first

    Models aren't done until they're deployed, monitored and retrained — CI/CD, blue/green releases, A/B tests and drift-aware feedback loops.

  • ◇

    Explainable & governed

    Knowledge graphs, SHAP and grounded retrieval so regulated teams can see why the system answered or flagged what it did.

  • ○

    Data quality is model quality

    A data-engineering foundation — PySpark, Databricks, Delta Lake, Snowflake — that keeps inputs clean, standardized and validated.

  • △

    Measured in business terms

    Handle time, escalations, offer acceptance, false positives, hours of manual work saved — the numbers stakeholders care about.
